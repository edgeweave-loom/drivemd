# DriveMD design

DriveMD looks and behaves like Google's own Workspace apps, such as Docs or Slides, rather than like GitHub: [Material Design 3](https://m3.material.io), [Google Sans Flex](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex) and [Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons). Its own color is the teal of its icon, as Docs has its blue and Sheets its green, on Google Drive's near-neutral greys. It never uses Google's logos or product icons, nor a name that could pass for a Google product, as the [Google Workspace Marketplace branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/workspace/marketplace/terms/branding) require; it names Drive only to say what it works with.

This file is the reference for milestone 9 of the spec's build plan, which brings the app to it. The spec says what the app does; this file says how it looks. The tokens are in [`src/tokens.css`](../src/tokens.css), which the app imports, and the mockups, drawn on made-up data, in [`design/mockups/`](design/mockups/):

## Content

- The interface is in English, in sentence case everywhere: buttons, headings, menu items and badges ("Move to trash", "Shared with me", "Read only").
- Drive's things keep Drive's words: My Drive, Shared drives, Shared with me, Shortcuts, trash, shortcut, folder. A Markdown file is a note in running text, and keeps its file name, extension included, in lists.
- The user is "you". Buttons are verbs ("Save", "Move", "Save mine as a copy"); a status is a past participle ("Saved", "Moved to trash").
- Messages say what happened and what to do, without apology: "This file is in the trash. Restore it from Google Drive to open it."
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no title case.

Touch and mouse are told apart by the pointer (`pointer: coarse`), never by the browser's name, and the app added to a phone's home screen by `display-mode: standalone`, where iOS shows no Back button of its own.

## Color

The tokens are Material Design 3's color roles. Each has a light and a dark value. On a phone or a tablet, the app follows the system's theme; on a computer, it keeps the light theme, as Docs, Sheets and Slides do on the web, so that a note looks like the Drive and Docs tabs beside it. A computer is told by its pointer, as touch is: the primary pointer is not coarse. The app has no switch of its own.

- Surfaces are near-neutral greys with a hint of blue, as in Google Drive; teal is for accents only. On a wide screen the app's background is `app-background`, and notes, editors and panels sit on `sheet`, white in light, a step lighter than the background in dark, so that a sheet reads as raised in both. A phone runs on `surface`.
- Text is `on-surface`, and secondary text (dates, the account, placeholders, inactive icons) `on-surface-variant`: both hold 7:1 or more on every surface, in both themes.
- `primary` is for the one filled action of a view, links, the focus ring and the current item's text; tonal actions are `secondary-container` with `on-secondary-container`.
- `attention-container` is for a notice that needs a decision (a conflict, unsaved changes to restore), `error-container` for a failure, and `error` only for actions that lose data. Material 3 has no warning role: the amber attention colors are harmonized toward the teal.
- What a version adds is underlined on `diff-added`, what it removes struck through on `diff-removed`: never told by color alone.
- `brand-mark` is the icon's own teal, kept for the icon's plate. No text or control takes it.
- Highlighted code takes the same roles, with no hue of its own: keywords and types `primary`, strings, numbers and literals `tertiary`, attributes, properties and variables `secondary`, comments `on-surface-variant` in italic, and names of functions, classes and sections the text's color at 600, in the viewer and in Visual's code blocks alike. Each holds 5:1 or more on a code block, `surface-container-high`, in both themes.

## Space, shape and depth

- Everything sits on a 4 px grid, `--space-1` (4 px) to `--space-7` (48 px). Page gutters are 16 px on a phone and 24 px wider.
- Controls are pills (`--radius-full`); text fields and code blocks take `--radius-sm` (8 px), banners, sheets' top corners and the note's editor `--radius-md` (12 px), panels `--radius-lg` (16 px), dialogs and the search bar `--radius-xl` (28 px).
- On a wide screen, a note shows as Docs shows a page: a sheet 816 px wide, 64 px of padding above and 72 px at the sides, `--elevation-1`, down to the window's bottom. In Visual, the note is edited on that one sheet, its lines from edge to edge of it, their text 72 px in, as Viewing's. In Source, the source and the preview are two sheets side by side, sharing the width, 32 px of padding at their sides (24 px on a tablet), each headed by its label-large name in `on-surface-variant` with its icon, `code` Markdown and `visibility` Preview. The source has no frame of its own: its lines run from edge to edge of the sheet, or of a phone's screen, their text as far in as the text beside it, and the cursor's line shows on `surface-container-low` while it has the focus, as in Docs, marked at its start by a 2 px `primary` rule, rather than a ring. What heads the note, its banners and badges, lines up with the sheet, or spans both while editing.
- Only what floats is lifted: `--elevation-1` for a sheet, a hovered filled button and a focused search bar, `--elevation-2` for menus and the app bar once the page scrolls under it, `--elevation-3` for dialogs, the snackbar and the floating buttons. Everything else is flat, set apart by its surface rather than by borders.

## States and focus

- Hover lays the content's color over a control at 8%, focus and press at 10%.
- The focus ring is a solid 2 px `primary` outline, 2 px outside the control: at least 5:1 on every surface in light, 7:1 in dark.
- A disabled control is `on-surface` at 38% on 12%, and only for an action that will come back. Save's "Saved" is a status, not a disabled control: it stays legible.
- Motion uses Material's standard easing, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, over 150 to 300 ms, and drops to nothing when the system asks for reduced motion.

## Touch

- Every control is at least 48 px (`--touch-target`) on a touch screen; a button can stay 40 px tall (`--control-height`) with an invisible margin. The keyboard toolbar's keys are 44 px, but 40 px wide for Undo, Redo and the table's in a table's cell, so that more of them show, and wider where the row scrolls, so that the last one in view is cut in the middle of its icon.
- Nothing works on hover alone.
- Bars at the screen's edges add `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to their padding: the app bar under the iPhone's status bar, the floating buttons and the snackbar above the home bar, and the keyboard toolbar's keys clear of the notch (over the keyboard, it needs no home bar's inset).

## Components

Measurements are in CSS pixels; colors are tokens.

- **App bar.** 64 px, or 56 px on a phone, on `app-background` (wide) or `surface` (phone). In the file navigator: the mark and "DriveMD", leading Home, lined up with the navigation drawer; the search bar; the account. On a note: the mark, the note's name with its Move button and the last-modified line under it, then the mode menu, Save, More actions (Move to trash) and the account. On a phone: Back or Done, the note's name, Save and More actions; on Home, the mark, "DriveMD", Search and the account; on a folder or a root, such as My Drive, Back, its name, Search and the account, as on Home, since a folder has no actions of its own for More actions to hold (`phone-folder` still shows More actions there). Opened from Drive, the mark leads nowhere, and takes Back's place on a phone, where More actions holds the account's items after the note's.
- **Sign-in.** Before sign-in, a card on `sheet`, `--radius-xl`, 48 px of padding and 448 px at most, over `app-background` on a wide screen, or the whole screen on a phone, with the about page's link at its foot: DriveMD's mark at 56 px, the heading at 28 / 36, a sentence in `on-surface-variant`, **Sign in with Google** filled across the card, then the about page's link. In a tab opened from Drive, the heading is "Open a note from Google Drive", and a line under the button says that Google lets the user choose the account, starting with the one Drive used when Drive named it, as it does in a tab that Drive's New opened; the line leaves while the button offers Try again.
- **About page.** A page outside the app, on the page's column (768 px at most): the heading, an introduction whose last words link to the app, then a tonal **Close** button at the text's start, which shows once the page's script runs. **Close** goes back to the app, or closes the tab, or opens the app (see the spec, Sign-in).
- **Account.** A 32 px round button with the account's initial on `primary-container`, since Google gives DriveMD no photo, which opens a menu: the email address, About DriveMD, Sign out, the version running in `on-surface-variant`, and Reload to update, with its `refresh` icon, once a new version is served.
- **Buttons.** Pills 40 px tall, 24 px of side padding (16 px before an icon), label-large. Filled (`primary`) for the one main action; tonal (`secondary-container`) for frequent ones, such as New note and the mode menu; outlined (`outline` border) for the safe way out of a choice; text for the least weight; danger (`error`) only to confirm a loss. Icon buttons are 40 px circles, their name in `aria-label` and a tooltip.
- **Save.** Always in the same place while the note can be edited, at least 112 px wide so that nothing beside it moves: "Saved" with `cloud_done` in `on-surface-variant`, without a container; "Save", filled; "Saving…", tonal with a spinner. Saved and Saving… are `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so that the button stays reachable and announces its state. While changes are unsaved, the browser tab's title starts with "• ".
- **Note's name.** It reads as text and renames the file when clicked: an `outline-variant` outline on hover, the focus ring while typing, Enter or leaving the field to rename, Escape to cancel. The extension stays outside the field in `on-surface-variant`.
- **Mode menu.** A tonal button naming the current mode (`edit` Visual, `code` Source, `visibility` Viewing) and a menu of the three, each with a line that says what it does: "Edit the note as it reads", "Edit the Markdown beside its preview", "Save, then read the note". The current mode is `secondary-container`, announced as a checked radio item. Wide screens only.
- **Floating Edit button.** On a phone, while reading a note that can be edited: 56 px, `--radius-lg`, `primary-container`, at the bottom right, 16 px from the edges plus the home bar's inset. The folder's New note button takes the same shape, with its label.
- **Visual, Source and Preview.** While editing on a phone: a segmented button, 40 px, outlined, cut in three, the chosen segment `secondary-container` with a check. It sits right above the note it switches, under the banners, which are about the whole note, and stays during a conflict, so that the user can check the rendered note before choosing.
- **Navigation drawer.** 256 px on `app-background`: Home, My Drive, Shortcuts, Shared drives, Shared with me, then a Vaults label and one item per vault; items are 40 px pills, the current one `secondary-container` with a filled icon. **Navigation rail**, between 768 and 1023 px: 80 px wide, 56 × 32 px pill indicators over 12 px labels, the vaults behind one Vaults item, which opens a menu of them beside it.
- **Panel.** Beside the drawer or the rail: a `sheet` with 16 px top corners, 24 px of padding, holding the page's heading, breadcrumbs on a folder, and its list.
- **File table.** On a wide screen, a folder's, Recent's and the search's items as table rows, 48 px tall, each one link with an `outline-variant` rule under it: Name (icon, name, badges), then Modified ("Sep 1, 2026, by Ada Lovelace"); Recent and search add Location, the item's folder, filled in as Drive answers. Folders come first in a folder, as today; Recent and search keep their order by date.
- **List row.** On a phone: 56 px, or 72 px with the line under the name (where it sits and when it changed, or why a shortcut is broken), an icon, the name wrapping anywhere rather than overflowing, a chevron for what opens a list, and no rule under it, nor under the app bar above the list. The folder pane beside a note uses dense rows, 40 px (48 px on touch), the open note `secondary-container`.
- **Breadcrumbs.** On folder pages only: the path to the folder, ending at it, after Home on a phone alone, where no drawer or rail leads there ("Home › My Drive › Work", "My Drive › Work" beside them); `on-surface-variant` steps, each a 32 px pill within a 44 px target, which shows under the mouse and which the focus ring rings, the current one `on-surface` at 500 and not a link, `chevron_right` between them.
- **Search.** On a wide screen, a 48 px pill on `surface-container-high`, at most 720 px wide, `sheet` with `--elevation-1` while focused. On a phone, an icon that opens a full-screen search on `surface-container-high`, with Back and Clear.
- **Badge.** 20 px, `--radius-xs`, label-medium: neutral (outline) for what an item is (Shortcut), `secondary-container` for where it belongs (Vault), `attention-container` for what the user cannot do (Read only).
- **Banner.** Under the app bar, `--radius-md`, 16 px of padding: an icon, a title-medium title, one or two sentences, and its actions. `attention-container` for a decision, `error-container` for a failure, `secondary-container` for a tip, such as how to add DriveMD to the Home Screen, whose one action, Got it, is a text button, or a new version of DriveMD, with its `refresh` icon and two text buttons, Reload then Later. The conflict's actions go by risk: Save mine as a copy (filled), Overwrite with mine (outlined), Keep the Drive version (danger text, which asks again).
- **Differences.** Google Sans Code at 14 / 22 on `sheet`, lines padded 12 px; unchanged runs folded into a `surface-container` line ("14 unchanged lines"). A changed line shows twice, Drive's then the user's, each on its own color, with only the characters that changed struck through or underlined; a line that only one version holds is struck through or underlined whole.
- **Snackbar.** `inverse-surface`, `--radius-xs`, `--elevation-3`, at the bottom left (bottom on a phone): one past-tense sentence ("Saved to Google Drive") and at most one action in `inverse-primary`, gone after 4 seconds, 10 with an action.
- **Dialog.** `surface-container-high`, `--radius-xl`, `--elevation-3`, over a `scrim` at 32%: the question as its title, one or two sentences of consequence, then Cancel and the action named by its verb, a loss in danger.
- **Keyboard toolbar.** `surface-container`, 48 px, 44 px keys that scroll sideways, as the spec describes it, widened so that the last one in view is cut in the middle of its icon, under a 32 px shade from `surface-container` to transparent over each end that hides keys; its background runs on below it to the screen's bottom. In a table's cell, its keys go in groups between 24 px rules in `outline-variant`, 2 px from the keys, Undo, Redo and the table's 40 px wide before they widen, the column's alignment on `secondary-container`, and Table options after a last rule, at the row's right end, where it does not scroll.
- **Formatting toolbar.** With a mouse or a trackpad, while editing: over the note, under the app bar, where it stays as the note scrolls, on the app's background; as wide as the note's sheet in Visual and both sheets in Source. A `surface-container` bar, 20 px corners and 4 px by 8 px of padding, of 32 px buttons, `--radius-xs`, their 20 px icons in `on-surface-variant`, in groups between 20 px rules in `outline-variant`: Find in note, Undo and Redo; Style, a 120 px button naming the cursor's line's style in 14 px `on-surface`, `arrow_drop_down` at its end, which opens a menu of the styles under it, 32 px items, the line's checked in `secondary-container`, each key at its end in `on-surface-variant`, weight 400; Bold, Italic, Strikethrough, Code and Link; Bulleted list, Numbered list, Checkbox and Quote; Outdent and Indent. A button's tooltip, the browser's own, says its name and its key. A button whose formatting holds the cursor shows pressed, in `secondary-container`. On a sheet too narrow for one row, the buttons wrap onto a second. In a table's cell, the groups of lines give way to the keyboard toolbar's table keys, as 32 px buttons, the column's alignment pressed and those that do not apply in `on-surface` at 38%, then **Table**, a button as Style is, without its width, which opens the table's menu below it. Find in note's bar sticks under it.
- **Table menu.** In Visual, for a table's cell: a menu as the others: Insert line break, alone above a rule, then Obsidian's items in three groups between rules: Add row above, Add row below, Move row up, Move row down, Delete row; Add column to the left, Add column to the right, Move column left, Move column right, Delete column; Align left, Align center, Align right, the column's own on `secondary-container`, announced as a checked radio item. An item that does not apply is off, in `on-surface` at 38%. On a touch screen, the keyboard toolbar's **Table options** key (`more_vert`), held at the row's right end while a cell is edited, opens it 4 px above the key, its right edge 8 px in from the screen's, with the items the toolbar lacks, 48 px tall and without the shortcut: Insert line break; Add row above, Move row up, Move row down; Add column to the left, Move column left, Move column right. It scrolls where the room between the app bar and the toolbar is shorter. With a mouse, the formatting toolbar's **Table** button opens it 4 px below the button, with the same items, 32 px tall, Insert line break showing its shortcut, Shift+Enter, at its end in `on-surface-variant`, weight 400.
- **Find in note.** While editing, one row: the field, a 40 px pill on `surface-container-high` at the editor's 16 px, `search` before it on a wider screen where the bar is 480 px wide or more; the count in body-medium `on-surface-variant` ("3 of 14", "14 matches", "No matches"); Previous and Next match; and Find options, a menu of Match case, Whole word and Regular expression, each checked while on, then Replace, which adds a second row: the Replace with field, then Replace and Replace all as text buttons. ## In the app

- The tokens are `src/tokens.css`, which `src/index.css` imports, and the editor's theme takes the same ones through CodeMirror's constructed style sheets. Obsidian's callouts and highlights keep Obsidian's own colors, and callouts its Lucide icons, as the spec says, in Viewing and in Visual alike: a callout's color is its type's token, `--callout-<type>` in `src/index.css`, which the editor's shadow root inherits.
- The security policy loads nothing from other sites: the fonts ship with the app as woff2 subsets, with their license in `LICENSES/`, and icons are inline SVG rather than an icon font, which would also show its ligature words while it loads.
- No inline `style` attribute: the policy blocks them.
