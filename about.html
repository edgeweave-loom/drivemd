About DriveMD

DriveMD lets people at Edgeweave browse, view and edit the Markdown files in their Google Drive, on a computer or a phone. It is an internal tool: only accounts of our Google Workspace organization can sign in.

Open DriveMD

Privacy

DriveMD has no server of its own. It runs in your browser, which talks to Google directly: what your notes hold goes between your browser and Google Drive, and nowhere else.

  • What it reads. DriveMD can reach everything you can in Google Drive, shared drives and files shared with you included, which it needs to edit files that other tools made. It lists what you browse: the files you viewed last, your folders, shared drives, files shared with you and shortcuts. It reads the notes you open, the images and notes they show or link to, the settings of an Obsidian vault, and your account's email address, to show who is signed in. It searches Drive by name when you search or follow a link, and for Obsidian vaults.
  • What it changes. Only what you ask: it saves a note, and creates, renames, moves or trashes a file. It also marks the files you open as viewed, so that they show among your recent ones. Before it saves over a version it did not write, such as the one you opened, it keeps that version in the note's version history until someone deletes it there, even if you then remove text from the note.
  • What stays on your device. The tab's sign-in, which lasts about an hour, for that tab only; your account's email address, so that it can offer Continue next time; and the text you have not saved yet, with the note's name, until you save or discard it. Signing out removes them for your account, in that browser. Your browser's history keeps the addresses of the pages you opened, which can name a Drive file or folder.
  • What it does not do. DriveMD sets no cookies of its own, has no analytics or ads, and shares nothing with anyone. Firebase Hosting, Google's web host, serves the app's files: it sees the address of each page you load, which can name a Drive file or folder, or hold what you share with DriveMD from another app on Android, and your browser's network address, as any web host does, but never what your notes hold.

Signing out does not remove DriveMD's access to your Google account. Our Google Workspace administrator installs DriveMD for the organization and can remove it.

Terms of service

Edgeweave provides DriveMD to its people for their work, as is, without any warranty. Your use of it, and of the files it opens, follows Edgeweave's policies and our Google Workspace terms.

DriveMD never rewrites a file you did not edit, and it keeps the version it saves over. Google Drive's version history is the place to check or undo a change.

Support

Report a problem or ask for a change in DriveMD's GitHub issues. Say what you did and what you saw, but leave out page addresses, file and folder names and what your notes hold: the project may become public.
